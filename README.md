# Sons & Letras — Business & Systems Analysis Case Study 🇩🇪

> A business and systems analysis case study based on a real German language education business I founded and manage.

## 📌 About the Project

**Sons & Letras** is an independent education business focused on German language learning for adults who are preparing to live and work in Germany.

The main audience includes:

- Professionals preparing to enter the German job market;
- Adults relocating to Germany and seeking better social and cultural integration;
- Professionals who need German language certification for their relocation or professional recognition process;
- Learners who need German for professional communication and everyday life in Germany.

This case study documents the current business model, customer journey and operational processes of Sons & Letras, while exploring how technology could improve and automate parts of the business.

---

## 🎯 Business Context

German language learning can be an important part of the professional migration process.

Throughout my professional experience, I have worked with adults preparing to relocate to Germany, including healthcare professionals participating in international recruitment and migration programs.

This experience helped me identify recurring needs such as:

- Language certification preparation;
- Professional communication in German;
- Job interview preparation;
- Workplace vocabulary;
- Cultural adaptation;
- Everyday communication in Germany;
- Continuous support throughout the learning journey.

Sons & Letras provides personalized German language education based on the learner's professional and personal goals.

---

## 👤 Target Users

### Primary User

An adult learner who:

- Plans to move to Germany;
- Needs German for professional purposes;
- May need to obtain a language certificate;
- Needs a flexible learning schedule;
- Wants personalized support;
- Is preparing for professional and social integration in Germany.

### Example User Journey

1. The learner discovers Sons & Letras.
2. The learner visits the landing page.
3. The learner contacts the business.
4. Their goals, German level and availability are identified.
5. A learning plan is defined.
6. Classes are scheduled.
7. Payment and administrative processes are completed.
8. Lessons begin.
9. Progress is monitored and the learning plan is adjusted when necessary.
10. The learner progresses toward their language, professional or integration goal.

## 🔎 Current Business Process

The current operation involves several different activities:

- Lead acquisition through a landing page;
- Initial communication with potential students;
- Understanding the learner's goals and needs;
- Student onboarding;
- Scheduling;
- Contract management;
- Payment management;
- German language instruction;
- Progress monitoring;
- Communication and follow-up.

Some of these processes still require manual administrative work.

---

## ⚠️ Problems & Opportunities

As a small education business, several operational activities depend directly on the business owner.

Potential challenges include:

- Manual communication with leads and students;
- Scheduling management;
- Payment follow-up;
- Repetitive administrative tasks;
- Information distributed across different tools;
- Difficulty scaling personalized service without increasing administrative workload.

These challenges create opportunities for process redesign and automation.

---

## 💡 Digital Transformation Opportunities

Possible future improvements include:

### Student Management

A centralized system could store:

- Student information;
- German proficiency level;
- Professional background;
- Learning goals;
- Certification goals;
- Class history;
- Learning progress.

### Automated Onboarding

A digital onboarding flow could collect:

- Contact information;
- Current German level;
- Professional goals;
- Target country/city;
- Certification requirements;
- Availability.

### Scheduling

Students could view available lesson times and manage bookings through an integrated scheduling system.

### Payment Automation

Recurring payments and automated payment notifications could reduce manual administrative work.

### Learning Progress Dashboard

Students could eventually access information about:

- Current level;
- Completed lessons;
- Learning objectives;
- Teacher feedback;
- Progress toward certification goals.
