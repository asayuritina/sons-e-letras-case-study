# Current Business Process (AS-IS)

## 1. Lead Acquisition

Sons & Letras has acquired prospective students through different channels.

The channels that have successfully generated paying students include:

- Organic referrals from current or former students;
- Preply, an online language learning platform.

A paid marketing campaign connected to a dedicated landing page was also tested.

Although the campaign generated a significant number of leads, most of them showed low purchase intent and did not convert into students.

The exact causes of the low conversion rate have not yet been determined and represent an opportunity for future analysis.

---

## 2. Initial Contact

When a prospective student contacts Sons & Letras, the first interaction focuses on introducing the service and understanding the student's situation.

The instructor introduces her professional background, qualifications and experience and explains how the service may support the prospective student's goals.

The conversation then moves into a needs assessment.

---

## 3. Needs Assessment

The prospective student is asked about:

- Their current German level;
- Whether they have studied German before;
- How long they have studied German;
- Previous experience learning other foreign languages;
- Their main reason for learning German;
- Whether they plan to live or work in Germany;
- Whether they need an official language certification;
- The purpose of the certification;
- Their expected timeline;
- Whether a certification exam has already been scheduled.

Previous foreign language learning experience can also help evaluate whether the prospective student has realistic expectations about the language learning process.

---

## 4. Goal and Timeline Analysis

Based on the information collected, the student's current situation is compared with their desired outcome.

Examples may include:

- Reaching a specific CEFR level;
- Preparing for a language certification;
- Developing German for professional communication;
- Preparing for relocation to Germany;
- Improving German for social integration.

The feasibility and estimated time required to reach the goal are discussed with the prospective student.

---

## 5. Learning Plan Proposal

Based on the student's:

- Current level;
- Previous language learning experience;
- Professional or personal objective;
- Certification requirements;
- Available preparation time;

an appropriate learning approach and expected timeline are discussed.

The objective is to align the learning plan with both the student's goals and realistic expectations about language acquisition.
